## "Favorite Scene"

## Kagami vs. Aomine: The Ultimate Battle of the Zone

The Winter Cup rematch between Seirin High and Touou Academy reaches a legendary climax when Aomine enters the Zone—a state of absolute mental and physical flow. Just when it seems Touou will break away, Kagami forces his way into the Zone to match him. The subsequent sequence features incredible lightning-fast animation as both aces tear across the court, trading high-flying dunks and impossible blocks in a flawless clash of wills.

## "Favorite Scence"

## The "Sick" Confession (Episode 4)

This is widely celebrated as one of the sweetest moments in the entire series. While Hori is bedridden with a high fever, she falls into a semi-conscious flashback about her childhood trauma of being left alone. As Miyamura prepares to leave for the night, he sits by her bed and casually drops his confession: "I'm in love with you." Thinking she is fast asleep, he leaves, unaware that Hori heard every word but was too flustered to move.
